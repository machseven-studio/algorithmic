Privacy Policy

Algorithm.ic · Institute Operations Platform

1. What we collect

When an Institute uses Algorithm.ic, we store: (a) account information for the Institute and any staff logins it creates (name, email, hashed password); (b) operational data entered into the workspace, which may include student names and batch/class assignments, teacher/staff names and subjects, invigilator names, and attendance records including stated reasons for absence; and (c) basic technical logs (timestamps, error logs) needed to run and maintain the Service.

2. Whose data this includes

Some of the data entered by an Institute relates to students, who may be minors. The Institute is the party directly interacting with students/guardians and is responsible for having appropriate notice/consent in place for entering that data into a third-party tool like ours, consistent with the DPDPA and any institutional policy that applies.

3. How we use this data

Data is used solely to operate the Service for the Institute that entered it: generating seating charts, timetables, duty rosters, and attendance PDFs, and powering the in-app assistant. We do not sell this data, and we do not use one Institute's data to serve another.

4. The AI assistant and third parties

The Assistant module sends the current seating grid, timetable, and duty roster state — along with your typed request — to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. No student attendance records, invigilator personal details beyond names already in that data, or account credentials are sent to Gemini beyond what's already part of those three data structures. Consult Google's own terms for how Gemini processes API requests.

5. Storage and security

Passwords are stored using salted PBKDF2 hashing, never in plain text. Data is stored in a database on our hosting provider (Render) and backed up automatically. Access to the workspace is restricted to authenticated sessions scoped to that Institute's account and any staff logins it has created.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain Institute data for as long as the account remains active, plus a limited backup retention window after deletion or cancellation. An Institute may request deletion of its data by contacting us; some data may persist briefly in backups until they naturally roll off.

7. Your rights

Institutes (and, through them, the individuals whose data they've entered) may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data held about them, subject to what's technically and legally feasible.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be communicated to account owners.
